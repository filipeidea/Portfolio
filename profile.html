Profile

Architect & Urbanist

I’m an architect and urbanist focused on concept design and architectural development. I enjoy turning complex briefs, site conditions and constraints into thoughtful architectural solutions.

My experience spans residential, hospitality, commercial and mixed-use projects, combining creative thinking with technical understanding and visual communication. Since 2021 I have worked in the concept team at Architects Office in São Paulo, on projects in Brazil and Portugal — from feasibility studies and schematic design to coordination with engineering consultants. Hand sketching remains central to how I think, present and tell the story of a project.

Originally from Rio Grande do Sul, I studied Architecture and Urbanism at UNISINOS, with an exchange at the Universidad Nacional de Mar del Plata, Argentina.
