Filipe Meltzer

Sketchbook

Thinking with the hand

Drawing is how I begin. These freehand sketches were made to read existing architecture, test spatial ideas, brief rendering artists and tell the story of a project — most of them during my work at Architects Office.

01 · For the project Quinta, Portugal

Reading Portuguese architecture through time

Before designing the Quinta, I drew the architecture of Portugal — monasteries, palaces, squares, chimneys, tiles and openings — to understand its proportions and details and reinterpret them in a contemporary estate.

See the Quinta project

02 · Multi-family project

Concept sketches for a housing scheme

Freehand creative process for a multi-family project: from a single dwelling with its gardens and terraces to the whole terraced block on its sloping site.

03 · Visual studies

Drawing the atmosphere before the render

Hand-made graphics requested for a highly complex project, used to guide the renderings in its early stages: the character of the landscape, the planting and the life of a public plaza.

04 · Storytelling

A project told in four drawings

A sequence of drawings used to tell the concept of a project: rocks, a tree growing among them, and the building volumes that follow.

05 · Hotel Barrinhas, Ceará

Arranging a hotel on the dunes

Early sketches for the arrangement of guest buildings, pools and gardens between the dunes and the sea.

See the Hotel Barrinhas project
